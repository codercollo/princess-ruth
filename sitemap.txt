GLOBAL CHROME (present on every route)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  TheProsceniumFrame   Fixed 1px brass hairline inset 14px from the viewport
                       edge, with a small bronze filigree mark at each corner.
                       Hidden below 768px (mobile gets a top rule only).
                       pointer-events: none, aria-hidden.

  TheHeader            Transparent over the hero, then gains a translucent
                       bògòlan-black backdrop-blur band after 80px of scroll.
                       Left: wordmark. Centre: nav (desktop ≥1024px).
                       Right: WhatsApp pill + hamburger (below 1024px).

  TheMobileDrawer      Full-screen. Opens as a curtain pull from the right,
                       revealing nav items that stagger up behind it.
                       Traps focus. Closes on route change and on Escape.

  TheTapeMeasure       Fixed left edge, desktop only. A vertical tape with cm
                       ticks; a brass needle marks scroll position. Hidden for
                       reduced-motion and below 1280px.

  TheCurtain           Fixed overlay, z-top. Two panels (maroon + indigo with
                       woven pattern) that part on first load and sweep across
                       on route change. See component-inventory.txt.

  TheFooter            Four columns collapsing to one. Address, hours, quick
                       links, socials, WhatsApp CTA, copyright.

  TheStickyContactBar  Mobile only (<768px). Fixed bottom bar, two buttons:
                       "WhatsApp" and "Call". Appears after 40% scroll depth.
                       This is the single most important conversion element on
                       the site — do not let the design swallow it.

1. HOME — /
================================================================================
Job: in 8 seconds, communicate that this is a real workshop making beautiful
clothes for real women, and put a WhatsApp button within thumb's reach.

  §1  OVERTURE / HERO
      The curtain pull resolves into: a full-bleed image of a garment under
      warm light in her shop. Headline set in the display face, mask-revealed
      line by line. One primary CTA (WhatsApp) + one secondary (See the work).
      A single quiet line of proof beneath: "{{SHOP_AREA}} · {{YEARS}} years ·
      six machines". Scroll cue at the foot.
      Motion: curtain pull (once per session), then line mask reveal.

  §2  WHAT SHE MAKES — "the rail"
      Four category panels laid out as garments hanging on a rail: unequal
      widths, not a 4-up grid of identical cards.
        Traditional & ceremonial · Official & office · Occasion & bridal ·
        Alterations & repairs
      Each links to the matching Gallery filter.
      Motion: panels settle in on first view, staggered, once. No hover lift.

  §3  THE SPOTLIGHT — one garment, one story
      Full-bleed. A single featured piece. Scrubbed spatial zoom pushes from
      the full garment into a detail macro (a hem, beadwork, a seam) as you
      scroll, while a short caption crossfades. This is the page's one bold
      moment. Everything either side of it is quiet.
      Motion: scrubbed scale + clip. Reduced-motion → static detail crop.

  §4  THE WORKSHOP — six machines, six hands
      Split layout: a shop interior frame on one side; on the other, three
      figures presented as facts, not as a stats card row:
        {{YEARS}} years at the machine · 6 machines running · 6 people employed
      Plus two sentences about how the shop actually works.
      Motion: counters tick up once on entry. Nothing else.

  §5  HOW A GARMENT IS MADE — the running order
      This IS a sequence, so numbering is justified here and nowhere else.
        1 Talk  2 Measure  3 Cut  4 First fitting  5 Finish
      Horizontal on desktop, vertical basting-stitch timeline on mobile.
      Each step gets one sentence and an honest time note.
      Motion: the basting-stitch line draws itself on scroll (stroke-dashoffset,
      scrubbed). Steps fade in against it.

  §6  SELECTED WORK
      6 portfolio pieces in the distorted grid treatment (a taste of Gallery).
      Ends with one link to /portfolio.
      Motion: column speed offset + velocity skew.

  §7  IN HER WORDS — kanga testimonials
      2–3 client quotes set on kanga-patterned bands, the way a kanga carries a
      printed proverb. Name + occasion beneath. Carousel on mobile, stacked
      offset bands on desktop.
      Motion: none beyond entry. Quotes don't need to move.

  §8  ALSO FROM THE SHOP
      A short, quiet band for jewellery and intimates. Two images, two lines,
      one link into the Gallery filter. Must not compete with the tailoring.

  §9  VISIT / CLOSING CALL
      Address, hours, a small map still, WhatsApp + Call buttons, directions
      link. Closes the page with the same warmth it opened with.
      Motion: the curtain edges ease back in from left and right at the page
      foot — the show closing. Subtle, 30% travel, not a full close.

2. GALLERY — /gallery
================================================================================
Job: browsing. Volume of work, fast filtering, one tap to WhatsApp about any
specific piece.

  §1  HEADER BAND
      Compact. Title + one line + live count ("64 pieces").

  §2  FILTER RAIL
      Chips, horizontally scrollable on mobile, sticky under the header.
        All · Traditional · Office & official · Occasion & bridal ·
        Bridal party · Repairs & restyles · Jewellery · Intimates
      Filtering is client-side and instant. Updates ?filter= in the URL so a
      filtered view is shareable. Uses GSAP Flip so items move rather than
      pop when the filter changes.

  §3  THE DISTORTED GRID
      Masonry-ish, deliberately irregular: 3 columns on desktop, 2 on tablet,
      1 on mobile. Columns scroll at slightly different speeds (0.92 / 1.0 /
      1.08) and skew by up to ~4deg based on scroll velocity, settling back to
      0 when scrolling stops. Card heights vary by a fixed set of aspect
      ratios (3:4, 1:1, 4:5, 2:3) assigned per item, not randomly at runtime
      — random heights cause layout shift on rehydration.

      CARD BEHAVIOUR
        Desktop hover → image dims slightly, a warm light pools from the top,
        title + fabric note rise, two buttons appear: WhatsApp · Call.
        Touch → the same two buttons are ALWAYS visible at the card foot.
        Keyboard → card is focusable; focus shows the same state as hover.
        The WhatsApp link prefills: "Hi {{TAILOR_SHORT}}, I'm interested in
        [piece name] from your gallery."

  §4  LOAD MORE
      12 per page, explicit button. Shows "Showing 24 of 64".
      New items animate in with Flip, not a full re-render.

  §5  CLOSING CTA
      "Didn't see it? Describe it and she'll make it." + WhatsApp.

3. SERVICES — /services
================================================================================
Job: answer "do you do X, how long, roughly how much" without a phone call.
Structured like a theatre programme's running order.

  §1  HEADER BAND

  §2  CUSTOM TAILORING
      Four service entries, each with: what it is, what's included, what to
      bring, typical turnaround, price treatment (range or "from" or
      "on request" — decide in the brief).
        - Traditional & ceremonial wear
        - Official & office wear
        - Occasion & event wear
        - Bridal & bridal party

  §3  ALTERATIONS & REPAIRS
      A compact table, not cards: service | typical turnaround | from-price.
        Hemming · Taking in / letting out · Zip replacement · Relining ·
        Strap & fastening repair · Restyle an existing garment

  §4  THE SHOP COUNTER
      Jewellery and women's intimates. Two short blocks, honest and brief.

  §5  HOW IT WORKS
      The same five steps as Home §5 but expanded — what happens at each
      stage, what she needs from the client, deposit and fitting policy.

  §6  QUESTIONS
      Accordion, 6–8 entries. Genuine questions: fabric, turnaround, deposits,
      remote measurements, rush jobs, what happens if it doesn't fit.
      Motion: accordion opens as a curtain draw (height + clip), 320ms.

  §7  CTA BAND

5. ABOUT — /about
================================================================================
Job: turn a business into a person. This page is what makes a stranger trust
sending money for a dress that doesn't exist yet.

  §1  PORTRAIT & OPENING
      Large portrait of {{TAILOR_NAME}} at the cutting table. Her name, and
      two sentences in first person. Not a mission statement.

  §2  HER STORY
      3–4 short paragraphs. Where she learned, when she opened, what changed,
      what she's proudest of. Offset serif text against a single image.
      Motion: line mask reveal once. Nothing else — this page should feel calm.

  §3  THE TEAM — six hands
      Six portraits, uneven grid, each with first name, role, years with her.
      If she'd rather not name them: one group frame + a paragraph about the
      team, and cut the grid.

  §4  THE WORKSHOP
      2–3 frames of the space and the machines, with a short caption each.
      "Six machines. Two overlockers. One very old Singer that outlives us all."

  §5  WHAT SHE STANDS BY
      Three short commitments — fit, honest timelines, fair work. Prose, not
      icon cards.

  §6  FIND THE SHOP
      Address block, hours, a map still, a "Get directions" deep link, and
      landmark-based directions (in Nairobi, landmarks beat street names:
      "opposite the stage, first floor above the chemist").

  §7  CTA BAND


================================================================================
6. CONTACT — /contact
================================================================================
Job: make it effortless to start a conversation, in the channel the visitor
already prefers. Channel order matters: WhatsApp first, call second, form last.

  §1  HEADER BAND — short, warm, one line.

  §2  CHANNELS
      Three blocks, WhatsApp given the most visual weight:
        WhatsApp   — primary, prefilled message, opens app on mobile
        Call       — tel: link, with hours beside it so nobody calls at 11pm
        Email      — mailto:, for diaspora and corporate enquiries

  §3  THE FORM
      Layout takes its cue from the reference image: a dark panel, underline-
      only inputs with a brass focus rule, generous spacing.
      Fields: Name* · Phone* · Email · What do you need? (select) ·
              When do you need it? (date, optional) · Your message*
      Honeypot field + timestamp check for spam. No CAPTCHA.
      POSTs to server/api/contact.post.ts → email via Resend/Postmark, plus a
      WhatsApp deep link shown on success ("Prefer to chat? Continue here").
      Motion: the submit button's label swaps for a stitching progress mark
      while sending. Success replaces the form with a short confirmation and
      the next-step link — it does not just show a green toast.

  §4  MAP
      Full-width. Leaflet + OpenStreetMap with a warm dark tile filter to match
      the palette, a custom brass pin, and a card overlay with the address and
      a "Open in Google Maps" link. Lazy-mounted only when scrolled into view.
      Fallback if the map fails to load: a static image + address + link.

  §5  VISITING
      Hours table, parking/matatu notes, landmark directions, "walk-ins
      welcome for repairs; fittings by appointment".


================================================================================
NAVIGATION RULES
================================================================================
  - Current route is marked with a brass underline and aria-current="page".
  - Every page ends with exactly one CTA band. Never two.
  - WhatsApp is reachable from every viewport at all times: header pill on
    desktop, sticky bottom bar on mobile.
  - Route change plays the curtain sweep (~700ms total, content swaps behind
    the closed panel at the midpoint). Nuxt's <NuxtPage> page transition hooks
    drive it: onLeave closes, onEnter opens.
  - Breadcrumbs only on /portfolio/[slug].
  - The logo always returns to /.
